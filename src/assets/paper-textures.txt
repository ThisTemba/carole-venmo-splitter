Paper textures, both from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0 (public domain).

paper-thermal.jpg: from "Paper 001" (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001), 1K color map.
paper-notepad.jpg: from "Paper 002" (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper002), 1K color map.

Processing (in the browser, on a canvas): cropped to a clean region (Paper 001's
right edge has print showing through from the back), mirrored four ways into a
seamless tile, converted to grey, and flattened to near white (mean 252) so the
paper color comes from the CSS and only the texture is multiplied onto it.

The app loads the .webp copies (re-encoded from the .jpg at quality 0.82 to cut download size); the .jpg files are the sources.
